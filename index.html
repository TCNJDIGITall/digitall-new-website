01 WHO WE ARE

A place to belong.
A space to grow.

DIGIT.all stands for Diversity & Inclusion Group In Tech for All. We believe the best tech communities make room for different stories, skills, and perspectives.

Through accessible workshops, conversations, guest speakers, and fun gatherings, we make tech feel a little more human.
